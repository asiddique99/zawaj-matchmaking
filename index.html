Privacy & safety

Your details stay with us

Marriage is personal. We treat your information with the amanah it deserves.

  • No public profiles. Nothing you submit is listed or searchable.
  • Consent first. We only share a summary with a potential match after you agree.
  • Photos on request. Pictures are shared only when both sides are ready.
  • Serious intentions only. Anyone not seeking nikah is removed.
  • Delete any time. Ask us and your registration is removed.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who can register?

Muslim men and women who are of legal marriageable age and sincerely seeking nikah. Parents or guardians may register on behalf of a son or daughter.

Is it free?

Registration is free. If we introduce any paid services in future, they will be clearly explained before you commit to anything.

How long until I hear back?

We review every registration personally. We'll contact you when we have a question or a promising introduction — quality matters more to us than speed.

Do you work with all backgrounds and madhabs?

Yes. Tell us your preferences on the form and we'll respect them in every introduction.

How do I update or remove my details?

Email us and we'll update or delete your registration. See our privacy notice.
